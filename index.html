LEGAL QUESTIONS & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

LEGAL QUESTIONS & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs) +

1. GENERAL LEGAL ASSISTANCE

Q1. What legal matters does Asoke Majumdar & Associates - Lexresource handle?

Asoke Majumdar & Associates - Lexresource handles matrimonial disputes, civil and criminal cases, property matters, arbitration, probate, succession, legal drafting, and suitable High Court proceedings, subject to the law and jurisdiction that apply.

Q2. What documents should I bring to a legal consultation?

Bring any relevant agreements, notices, court orders, letters or emails, identity documents, and other records connected with your matter. The documents needed may differ depending on the jurisdiction and type of proceeding.

2. MATRIMONIAL & FAMILY LAW

Q1. Do you handle divorce and matrimonial cases in Kolkata?

Yes, as an experienced matrimonial lawyer, we handle divorce by mutual consent, contested divorce, maintenance, domestic violence, and child custody matters with complete confidentiality.

Q2. How long does mutual divorce take in Kolkata?

A mutual consent divorce typically takes around 6-9 months, depending on the applicable legal requirements, the court's diary, and the dates permitted by the court. We assist with the preparation of documentation, drafting and filing the joint petition, and court appearances as required.

Q3. What is the difference between mutual and contested divorce?

In a mutual-consent divorce, both spouses agree to the divorce. In a contested divorce, the spouses disagree about the divorce or related issues, or one spouse does not give joint consent. The grounds, process, and legal terms may differ under the law that apply in the relevant jurisdiction.

Q4. Can I enforce a maintenance order?

Yes. The steps for enforcing a maintenance order depend on the applicable law, jurisdiction, and terms of the order.

3. PROPERTY & REAL ESTATE

Q1. What documents should I verify before purchasing property?

Check the title deeds, ownership records, details of any encumbrances, approved plans where required, and relevant registration documents. The records and checks required may differ depending on the location.

Q2. Does property mutation establish ownership?

No. Mutation is generally an entry in revenue or municipal records. By itself, it does not prove legal ownership or title. Its legal effect and the procedure for obtaining it may differ by jurisdiction.

4. CIVIL & CRIMINAL LITIGATION

Q1. What should I do after receiving a court summons?

Check the hearing date and any instructions in the summons, keep all related documents safe, and seek legal advice promptly. The required response and deadlines depend on the court and jurisdiction.

Q2. Can I seek an injunction in a property dispute?

Possibly. Whether an injunction is available depends on the facts, evidence, legal requirements, and the court's jurisdiction.

Q3. Is bail available in every criminal case?

Bail is not automatic in every case. It depends on the offence, applicable law, facts of the case, and jurisdiction. The procedure and legal terms may differ by location.

5. HIGH COURT & WRIT JURISDICTION

Q1. What is a writ petition?

A writ petition asks a court with the proper authority to grant a legal remedy. It must meet the court's jurisdictional and procedural requirements and comply with applicable law. The types of writs and related terms may differ by jurisdiction.

6. ARBITRATION & NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS

Q1. Can an arbitration award be challenged?

Yes, but only on grounds recognised by law and within the applicable legal time limits. The grounds, procedure, and deadlines depend on the governing law and jurisdiction.

Q2. What can I do if a cheque is dishonoured?

Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, may provide a remedy if all statutory requirements are met and the matter falls within the relevant jurisdiction. Notice requirements, time limits, and procedures may differ under the governing law and depending on the location.

7. SUCCESSION & LEGAL DOCUMENTATION

Q1. What is probate of a Will?

Probate is a court order or grant relating to a Will, where the law provides for it and requires it. Whether probate is needed and how to obtain it may differ by jurisdiction.

Q2. What is a Succession Certificate?

A Succession Certificate is issued by a court and mainly relates to specified debts and securities belonging to a deceased person. Its scope, availability, and procedure may differ under the law applicable in the relevant jurisdiction.

Q3. Can an advocate draft deeds, agreements, and Wills?

Yes. An advocate may help prepare these documents based on the transaction, the client's instructions, and applicable law. The required formalities, signing requirements, and registration procedures may differ by location.

8. CONSULTATION & FEES

Q1. How are legal fees determined?

Fees depend on the type, complexity, and scope of the work, the jurisdiction involved, and the terms agreed with the advocate.

Q2. Can an advocate guarantee the outcome of a case?

No. The result of a case depends on the facts, evidence, applicable law, jurisdiction, and the court's decision.

DISCLAIMER: These FAQs provide general legal information only. They do not constitute legal advice or create an advocate-client relationship. Each matter requires individual assessment. Procedures, terminology, deadlines, and legal requirements may vary by jurisdiction and location; obtain specific advice for the relevant court or authority.
